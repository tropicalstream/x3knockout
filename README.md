# X3 Knockout

X3 Knockout is an arcade boxing game for the RayNeo X3 Pro AR glasses, built in the spirit of classic stand-up-and-dodge boxing cabinets. A glowing caricature boxer stands in the ring in front of you, telegraphing every punch through his eyes, gloves, and posture before it lands, and you respond by physically slipping, ducking, or stepping with your own body while punching by tapping the temples of the glasses — both hands raised to the temples doubling as a boxer's guard. The game runs on a SUPERHOT-style time law: the world only moves as fast as you do, slowing to a near-stop when you're still and snapping forward the moment you commit to a dodge or a punch, so a telegraphed haymaker hangs in the air until your own motion decides when it arrives. Head tilt and nod are read directly from the glasses' motion sensors to infer where your body has moved, translating a lean into a slip and a nod into a duck or a body-shot stance without any external camera or controller.

## Controls

- Tap the left temple pad for a left punch, the right temple pad for a right punch
- Tap both pads within 120 ms for a special attack
- Swipe down and hold on the right pad to guard
- Swipe left or right on the right pad to step
- Tilt your head to slip, nod to duck or aim low
